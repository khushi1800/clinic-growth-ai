## Methodology

**Sources Used:**
1. Google Maps / Google Business Profile (public listings)
2. Practo.com (public clinic listings)
3. Clinic websites (publicly accessible)
4. JustDial public listings

**Qualification Score (1–10):**
- **8–10:** Strong fit — multi-doctor, active online, tech-forward
- **5–7:** Medium fit — growing clinic, may need education
- **Below 5:** Weak fit — too small or misaligned

**Recommended Approach:**
- **Hot:** Direct WhatsApp + personalized email same week
- **Warm:** Email first, WhatsApp follow-up after 3 days
- **Cold:** Email only, re-evaluate in 60 days

> ⚠️ **IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER:** The clinics below were identified from public listings at time of research. Clinic names, URLs, and details may change. VERIFY all information before outreach. Do NOT contact personal numbers. This list is a starting point for research, not a verified cold call list.

## Research Notes & Gaps

### What Was Found
- 40 clinics identified across 6 cities using public sources
- All entries verified against at least one public source (Google Business or Practo)
- Multi-doctor clinics were prioritized as they have more pain from call management and lead tracking

### Acknowledged Gaps
1. **Phone numbers:** Intentionally not collected (personal numbers excluded per privacy rules). Business phone numbers can be found on Google Business listings at time of outreach.
2. **Email addresses:** Not listed here. Business emails (if public) should be found on clinic websites at time of contact.
3. **Solo practitioners:** Not included but represent a large market — can be targeted in Phase 2 with lower-touch digital marketing.
4. **Tier-2 cities:** Not covered in this initial research. Consider adding Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Coimbatore, Lucknow in Month 3+.
5. **Verification date:** All research conducted November 2026. Clinic details change — always verify before outreach.

### Recommended Research Tool for Expansion
To scale beyond 40 clinics, use:
1. **Practo.com** — Filter by city > Dentist > Multi-speciality Clinic > sort by reviews
2. **Google Maps** — Search "dental clinic [city area]" and scroll through listings
3. **JustDial** — Similar filtering capability
4. For each prospect: note name, area, website (if any), Google rating, number of listed doctors

### Data You Should NOT Collect
- Personal mobile numbers of doctors (even if publicly visible on WhatsApp)
- Personal email addresses (use only office@, info@, contact@ business emails)
- Patient data of any kind
- Any information not publicly listed
