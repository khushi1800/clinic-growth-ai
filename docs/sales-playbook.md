## 2. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

### Primary ICP — Tier-1 Multi-Doctor Dental Clinic

| Attribute | Criteria |
|-----------|----------|
| **Location** | Metro cities: Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune |
| **Size** | 2–8 doctors; 3–10 chairs; 1–3 branches |
| **Revenue** | Estimated ₹15L–₹1Cr+ per month |
| **Tech Comfort** | Has website, Google Business Profile, possibly on Practo |
| **Pain Signals** | Active Google Ads spend, responsive on WhatsApp, reviews mention wait times |
| **Decision Maker** | Clinic owner/founder-dentist, not a corporate manager |
| **Budget Capacity** | Can invest ₹15,000–₹25,000/month in tools that pay for themselves |

### Secondary ICP — Growing Solo Practitioner

| Attribute | Criteria |
|-----------|----------|
| **Location** | Metros and Tier-2 cities (Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Lucknow, Coimbatore) |
| **Size** | 1 doctor + 1–2 support staff; 2–4 chairs |
| **Pain** | Does everything manually; losing leads when in consultation |
| **Budget** | Standard plan at ₹14,999/month is accessible if ROI is clear |

### Anti-ICP (Do NOT pursue)

- Government/public hospitals (procurement is slow, budget is locked)
- Dental colleges (academic context, different buying behavior)
- Very small single-chair setups in rural areas (willingness to pay is low)
- Corporate dental chains (Clove, Apollo Dental — they have internal systems)

## 3. Qualification Framework (BANT + Pain Score)

### BANT Assessment

| Dimension | Qualifying Questions | Score |
|-----------|----------------------|-------|
| **Budget** | "What do you currently spend on tools/software?" — if ₹5K+ they qualify | 1–3 |
| **Authority** | "Are you the owner/decision-maker?" — owner = 3, manager = 1 | 1–3 |
| **Need** | Pain score (see below) | 1–4 |
| **Timeline** | "When do you want to solve this?" — this month = 3, sometime = 1 | 1–3 |

**Total BANT Score: 1–13**
- **10–13:** Hot lead — prioritize demo booking
- **7–9:** Warm lead — nurture with 1–2 touchpoints
- **Below 7:** Park and re-engage in 60 days

### Pain Score Checklist (Ask directly or observe)

| Pain Indicator | Points |
|---------------|--------|
| Misses patient calls during consultations | +2 |
| No automated follow-up system | +2 |
| Staff manually enters every patient detail | +1 |
| Active Google Ads but no lead tracking | +2 |
| Loses patients to competitors after first inquiry | +2 |
| No appointment reminder system | +1 |

## 4. Common Objections & Responses

### Objection 1: "It's too expensive — ₹15,000/month is a lot."

**Response:**
> "I completely understand, Dr. [Name]. Let's do the math together. If your clinic misses even 10 calls a month — which most clinics do during consultations — and even half of those would have converted to appointments at ₹2,000 average... that's ₹10,000 lost every month. Our AI receptionist answers every call and follows up on WhatsApp automatically. Most of our clients see the system pay for itself within the first week. Would you like to see the ROI calculator?"

**Backup:** Offer a free 14-day pilot (see Pilot Offer section).

---

### Objection 2: "I don't trust AI to talk to my patients."

**Response:**
> "That's a really valid concern, and I'd have the same question. Our AI doesn't pretend to be a human — it introduces itself as your clinic's assistant and handles basic questions: timings, services, booking requests. It escalates anything complex to your staff immediately via WhatsApp. It's like having a 24/7 junior receptionist who never sleeps, never makes your patients wait on hold, and passes every important query to you instantly. Can I show you a live demo?"

---

### Objection 3: "We already use [Practo/WhatsApp/Excel] for this."

**Response:**
> "That's great — most of our clients also started with those tools. The challenge is that Practo doesn't follow up with leads who called but didn't book, WhatsApp requires manual replies at midnight, and Excel has no way to automatically remind patients about their appointment. Clinic Growth AI connects all of these together — we don't replace your current setup, we make it automated. Can I show you how clinics similar to yours have used this?"

---

### Objection 4: "What if the AI gives wrong information to my patients?"

**Response:**
> "Great question. The AI only answers based on FAQs and information *you* provide during setup. It won't guess. If a patient asks something outside of what you've trained it on, it immediately says 'Let me have our team follow up with you shortly' and alerts your staff via WhatsApp. You review and approve everything the AI says before it goes live. Full control stays with you."

---

### Objection 5: "I want to think about it / check with my partner."

**Response:**
> "Of course — this is an important decision. Can I ask what specific concern you'd like to discuss with them? That way I can prepare any information that might help. Also, our pilot offer is only valid for the next [X] days at the discounted rate — I'd hate for you to miss it while deliberating. Would a 15-minute call with both of you work this week?"

---

### Objection 6: "What happens if the AI makes a mistake?"

**Response:**
> "Every AI-to-patient interaction is logged and you can review it in your dashboard anytime. If you're unhappy with any response, you can update the FAQ, and the AI learns immediately. During the first 30 days, our team actively monitors your account and flags anything that looks off. And if something genuinely goes wrong, our SLA guarantees human escalation within 15 minutes during clinic hours."

## 5. Demo Flow & Key Selling Points

*(Full script in demo-script.md — this is the summary for sales reps)*

### Demo Order (30 minutes)
1. **Pain Hook** (3 min) — Ask about missed calls, show the revenue loss calculator
2. **Dashboard Overview** (5 min) — Clean, mobile-first; show at-a-glance KPIs
3. **AI Receptionist Live** (8 min) — Make a live test call; show WhatsApp auto-response
4. **Lead Management** (5 min) — Show lead pipeline, status changes, assignment
5. **Appointment System** (4 min) — Show booking, reminders, no-show tracking
6. **ROI Calculator** (3 min) — Plug in their clinic's numbers, show payback period
7. **Pricing & Next Steps** (2 min) — Present pilot offer

### Key Selling Points (ranked by impact for Indian market)
1. **Never miss a patient call again** — 24/7 AI answers
2. **ROI is measurable** — recovered leads pay for the subscription
3. **No staff retraining needed** — works alongside existing team
4. **WhatsApp-native** — fits Indian communication habits perfectly
5. **Setup in 7 days** — not a 3-month enterprise implementation
6. **Local Indian support** — not a US-based product with no support in IST

## 8. Closing Techniques (Indian Market Context)

### The Summary Close
After the demo: *"So to recap — you're currently missing roughly [X] calls a month, losing about ₹[X] in potential revenue. We can have your AI receptionist live in 7 days. The pilot offer is ₹7,499/month. Shall we get started?"*

### The Next-Step Close (Preferred)
Never ask "Do you want to buy?" — instead ask: *"What would you need to see before we schedule your onboarding call?"*

### The Deadline Close (Use sparingly)
*"Our founding-clinic pricing is available to the next 5 clinics only — we're at [X] right now. I'd hate for you to come back next week and find the price has gone up."*

### The Social Proof Close (Once you have clients)
*"[Clinic Name] in Bangalore had a similar hesitation. They went live 2 weeks ago and have already recovered 15 missed leads. Would it help if I connected you with their owner for a quick chat?"*

### Cultural Notes for Indian Sales Conversations
- **Relationship first:** Spend 5 minutes on personal connection before business
- **Respect hierarchy:** Always confirm you're speaking with the decision-maker (owner)
- **WhatsApp is preferred:** Follow up via WhatsApp, not just email
- **Payment flexibility:** Be ready to discuss quarterly upfront payment at a small discount
- **Festival timing:** Avoid hard closes during major festivals; use pre-festival period to pitch (clinics are busier, thinking about growth)
- **Regional language:** If possible, have materials in Marathi (Mumbai), Kannada (Bangalore), Tamil (Chennai) — even just the WhatsApp opener

### Where to Find Sales Contractors
- **LinkedIn:** Search "medical sales representative", "healthcare SaaS sales India"
- **Naukri.com:** Post for "Healthcare Sales Executive" (freelance/part-time)
- **Internshala:** Part-time sales roles if budget is tight
- **Referral:** Ask your pilot clinic owners — they know other clinic managers

### Compensation Structure
| Model | Base | Commission | When to Use |
|-------|------|------------|-------------|
| **Commission-only** | ₹0 | 15–20% of first 3 months MRR | Early stage, unproven |
| **Retainer + commission** | ₹15,000/month | 10% of MRR (ongoing) | After 5+ paying clients |
| **Full-time** | ₹25,000–₹40,000/month | 5–8% of MRR | After ₹2L+ MRR |

**Commission example:** Close a ₹14,999/month client → contractor earns ₹2,249/month ongoing (10% model) or ₹7,499 flat (20% first 3 months).

1. "Tell me about a healthcare product you've sold and how you found prospects."
2. "Walk me through how you'd approach a dental clinic owner on WhatsApp cold."
3. "What's your expected timeline to close your first deal?"
4. "How many calls/meetings can you do per week?"
