## Usage Guidelines

| Template | When to Use | Personalize With |
|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| Cold Email A | Clinics with active Google presence | Google rating, location |
| Cold Email B | Clinics with high booking volume | No-show problem angle |
| Cold Email C | Highly researched prospects | Specific clinic detail |
| Follow-up 1 | 3 days after no reply | Pilot offer highlight |
| Follow-up 2 | 7 days after no reply | Link to relevant content |
| Follow-up 3 | 14 days (final) | Graceful exit |
| WhatsApp Intro | First WhatsApp contact | Clinic name, location |
| LinkedIn | Connection requests | Professional, brief |
| Demo Confirmation | Immediately after booking | Meeting details |
| Post-Demo | Within 2 hours of demo | Pain points from call |
| Proposal | 24 hrs after demo | Customized numbers |
| Onboarding Welcome | Day of payment confirmation | Clinic name, timeline |
| Referral Request | After 30+ days as customer | Specific result achieved |
| Testimonial Request | After 60–90 days | Time with us, result |
