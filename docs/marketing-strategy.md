# Clinic Growth AI — 6-Month Marketing Strategy

> **Version:** 1.0 | **Created:** November 2026 | **Budget:** ₹20,000 initial
> **Market:** Indian dental clinics — Metro-first approach

---

## Marketing Goal

Acquire **25 paying dental clinic customers in 6 months** using a low-budget, high-touch strategy that builds trust through personal relationships before scaling to paid channels.

**North Star Metric:** Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)
**Secondary Metrics:** Demo calls booked per week, conversion rate from demo to paid

---

## Phase 1 — Months 1–2: Direct Outreach & Relationship Building

### Objective
Get the first 3–5 pilot customers. This phase is 95% founder-led, low-cost, and high-touch.

### Channel 1: Direct WhatsApp Outreach
- **Target:** 10 clinics per day from the prospect research list
- **Tool:** WhatsApp Business account (free)
- **Message:** See outreach-templates.md (WhatsApp opener)
- **Volume:** ~100 clinics contacted in Month 1 → aim for 15-20 demos booked
- **Cost:** ₹0 (WhatsApp is free)

### Channel 2: Dentist Facebook/WhatsApp Groups
- **Action:** Join dentist communities on Facebook, WhatsApp, and Telegram
  - "Indian Dental Association — Members Group"
  - State-level IDA WhatsApp groups
  - Dental alumni WhatsApp groups (BDS/MDS graduates)
- **Strategy:** Provide value first (share insights about clinic management, missed call stats) before promoting the product
- **Cost:** ₹0

### Channel 3: LinkedIn Direct Outreach
- **Target:** Dentists and clinic owners on LinkedIn — search "dental clinic owner India"
- **Volume:** 10 connection requests + personalized messages per day
- **Cost:** ₹0 (LinkedIn Basic); upgrade to Sales Navigator (₹2,999/month) if conversion warrants it

### Channel 4: Local Dental Association Networking
- **Action:** Attend local IDA (Indian Dental Association) chapter meetings
  - Mumbai: IDA Mumbai Branch — monthly meets
  - Bangalore: IDA Karnataka State Branch
  - Delhi: IDA Delhi State Branch
- **Cost:** ₹500–₹1,500 per event (registration/membership)
- **Goal:** 5–10 real conversations per event; collect business cards; follow up within 24 hrs on WhatsApp

### Channel 5: Google Business Profile for Clinic Growth AI
- **Action:** Create and fully optimize Google Business Profile for Clinic Growth AI
- **Cost:** ₹0
- **Why:** When dentists search "AI for dental clinic India," we appear in local results

## Phase 2 — Months 3–4: Content Marketing & Referral Engine

### Objective
Build inbound lead generation while converting warm referrals from pilot customers.

### Channel 1: Referral Program Launch
- **Ask every pilot customer:** "Do you know 2 dentists who'd benefit from this?"
- **Incentive:** 1 free month of subscription for every paying customer they refer
- **Process:** Create a simple referral link/tracking (can be as simple as a WhatsApp message: "Tell them [Clinic Name] sent you")
- **Cost:** ₹0 upfront (cost only if referral converts)

### Channel 2: Blog / Content Marketing
- **Platform:** Company website blog (or Medium as a free alternative)
- **Post cadence:** 2 posts per week

**Blog Topics (High-Intent for Dental Clinic Owners):**
1. "How Many Patient Calls Is Your Clinic Missing? (The ₹3 Lakh Hidden Drain)"
2. "5 Reasons Dental Patients Don't Come Back (And How to Fix It)"
3. "WhatsApp for Dental Clinics: The Right Way vs The Manual Way"
4. "AI Receptionist for Dental Clinics: Myth vs Reality"
5. "No-Show Appointments: How to Reduce Them by 40% with Automation"
6. "What Every Growing Dental Clinic Gets Wrong About Lead Management"
7. "Case Study: [Pilot Clinic Name] Recovers ₹X in Missed Leads in 30 Days"
8. "The ROI of Automated Patient Follow-ups (India-Specific Numbers)"

- **SEO Targets:** "AI for dental clinic India," "dental clinic management software," "patient follow-up automation dental"
- **Cost:** ₹0 (founder writes); or ₹3,000–₹5,000/post if outsourced

### Channel 3: Instagram for Dentists
- **Account:** @clinicgrowth.ai (or similar)
- **Content Type:** Carousels — "5 tools every dental clinic should use," "Before/After lead management setup"
- **Hashtags:** #DentalClinicIndia #DentistIndia #DentalBusiness #ClinicManagement
- **Target:** 500 followers by Month 4; 1–2 DM inquiries per week
- **Cost:** ₹0 (organic)

### Channel 4: First Case Study Publication
- **Action:** With pilot clinic's consent, write a detailed case study:
  - Problem → Solution → Results (leads recovered, revenue protected)
  - Publish on website, LinkedIn, share in dental groups
- **Cost:** ₹0

## Phase 3 — Months 5–6: Paid Advertising & Partnerships

### Objective
Scale acquisition with proven messaging. By this point, ROI data from pilot customers validates ad claims.

### Channel 1: Google Ads (Search)
- **Budget:** ₹10,000–₹15,000/month (from revenue now being generated)
- **Targeting:** Keywords — "dental clinic management software India," "AI receptionist for clinic," "patient follow-up automation"
- **Ad Copy Angle:** "Never Miss a Patient Call Again — AI Receptionist for Dental Clinics. Try Free."
- **Landing Page:** Dedicated page with ROI calculator and case study

### Channel 2: LinkedIn Ads (Sponsored InMail)
- **Budget:** ₹8,000/month
- **Targeting:** Job title "Dentist" or "Dental Clinic Owner" in Mumbai, Bangalore, Delhi NCR, Hyderabad
- **Format:** Sponsored InMail with case study link

### Channel 3: Dental Equipment Supplier Partnerships
- **Target Partners:** Equipment distributors and dental supply companies
  - **Henry Schein India** — large dental equipment distributor
  - **Dentsply Sirona India** — equipment manufacturer with large Indian dental client base
  - **Ivoclar India** — materials/equipment supplier with dentist relationships
- **Proposal:** Co-marketing — they introduce us to their clinic customers; we give their customers a discounted first month
- **Cost:** ₹0 (revenue share or flat referral fee)

### Channel 4: IDA Partnership — Chapter-Level
- **Action:** Approach IDA chapter executives with a formal partnership proposal
- **Offer:** Free 90-day trial for IDA member clinics; IDA gets co-branding rights
- **Goal:** Access to IDA's network of 30,000+ registered dentists across India
- **Cost:** ₹0 upfront; potential sponsorship of ₹5,000–₹15,000 for IDA events

### Channel 5: Dental Conferences & Exhibitions
- **Target Events:**
  - **IDA National Conference** (annual, usually Dec/Jan) — largest dental event in India
  - **AIDA (Association of Indian Dental Alumni)** events
  - Regional dental exhibitions in Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore
- **Booth/Stall:** ₹15,000–₹30,000 for stall space
- **Activity:** Live demos at the stall; collect contacts; offer pilot sign-up discount
