## 2. Detailed Month-by-Month Execution Plan

### Month 1 (December 2026): MVP Launch & First 3 Founding Pilots

#### Core Focus: Product-in-the-Wild Validation
- **Target Commercial Milestone:** 3 Signed Founding Pilot Clinics (Bengaluru / Mumbai).
- **Pricing:** Setup fee waived (₹9,999 value); 50% discount at ₹7,499/mo for first 60 days.
- **Key Objectives:**
  - Complete live deployment of the core web app and Supabase backend.
  - Finalize WhatsApp integration through an approved Indian BSP (Gupshup, AiSensy, Wati or Interakt) for automated incoming call fallbacks. *(Decision of record, 2026-11-28: BSP, not direct Cloud API; see `engineering-roadmap.md`.)*
  - Conduct hands-on onboarding for first 3 clinics using `customer-onboarding.md`.
  - Achieve 100% resolution of Day 1–7 technical bugs.
- **Deliverables:**
  - [ ] 3 Signed Founding Pilot agreements.
  - [ ] Live AI Receptionist handling after-hours inquiries at 3 partner clinics.
  - [ ] Weekly telemetry check: zero clinical hallucinations or incorrect scheduling.
- **Gate / Decision Point 1:** *Do patients engage with the AI receptionist without frustration?* If drop-off > 30%, refine the conversational prompt before adding more clinics.

### Month 5 (April 2027): Enterprise Tier & Dermatology Expansion Pilot

- **Deliverables:**
  - [ ] 3 dental clients upgraded to Premium tier.
  - [ ] 2 dermatology clinics live on pilot.

## 3. Key Decision Gates & Governance Milestones

```
GATE 1 (End of Month 1) ──► Product Viability (Triage & AI Safety)
GATE 2 (End of Month 2) ──► Paid Conversion & Zero Churn Benchmark
GATE 3 (End of Month 4) ──► Contractor Sales Repeatability (Non-founder sales)
GATE 4 (End of Month 6) ──► Scale Strategy (Bootstrap vs Pre-Seed Round)
```

| Gate | Timing | Evaluation Criteria | Green Light (Proceed) | Red Light (Contingency Action) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Gate 1** | End of Month 1 | Clinical safety & patient sentiment | AI handles ≥ 85% of queries without manual intervention | Roll back autonomous replies; switch to draft mode where staff clicks to approve AI replies before sending. |
| **Gate 2** | End of Month 2 | Conversion of pilot clinics to paying | ≥ 2 of 3 founding pilots pay standard subscription fee | Extend pilot by 14 days; conduct in-depth clinic workflow audit to discover friction points. |
| **Gate 3** | End of Month 4 | Sales contractor productivity | Contractor closes ≥ 3 independent deals in first 30 days | Replace contractor or adjust commission structure; refocus on founder-led webinars. |
| **Gate 4** | End of Month 6 | Profitability & Expansion readiness | MRR > ₹4L with net profit margin > 60% | Maintain lean bootstrapped operations; expand into Tier-2 cities (Jaipur, Lucknow, Surat). |

## 4. Risk Factors & Contingency Management Matrix

| Risk Domain | Specific Threat Scenario | Probability | Impact | Proactive Contingency Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Regulatory / Medical** | Patient claims AI provided unauthorized diagnostic consultation | Low | High | Strict conversational prompt constraints: AI identifies itself as an administrative assistant, explicitly states *"I am not a doctor"*, and offers only scheduling and logistics. |
| **Telephony / API** | Meta restricts WhatsApp Business API phone number due to spam reports | Low-Med | High | Only send messages to inbound users (user-initiated 24h customer care window) or transactional utility templates. Zero unsolicited bulk promotional broadcasts. |
| **Market Resistance** | Dentists hesitate to adopt recurring ₹15,000 SaaS expense | Medium | Medium | Maintain the "Payback in 6 Recovered Patients" ROI calculator in every proposal. If required, offer a performance-based pilot with a lower base fee + per-booking fee. |
| **Competitive Reaction** | Practo or Dentee releases an integrated AI receptionist module | Medium | High | Maintain faster iteration velocity and superior Indian conversational fidelity (Hinglish/voice nuance). Focus on clinic-specific customisation and white-glove local support that national incumbents cannot replicate. |
| **Key Man Dependency** | Over-reliance on founder for sales and onboarding | High | Medium | Documented standard operating procedures (`customer-onboarding.md` and `sales-playbook.md`) enable any new sales contractor or CSM to execute independently by Month 3. |
